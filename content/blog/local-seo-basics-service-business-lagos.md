# Local SEO Basics for a Service Business in Lagos

Local SEO works when your website and your profile tell the same clear story.

This guide gives a practical baseline for Lagos service businesses that want more qualified enquiries.

## 1) Start with local service intent, not generic traffic goals

Define the exact searches tied to real revenue:

- service + location intent
- urgent problem intent
- commercial comparison intent

Example pattern:

- "dentist in Lekki"
- "AC repair Victoria Island"
- "wedding photographer Lagos price"

This keeps your strategy focused on buyers, not vanity traffic.

## 2) Align website pages with profile categories

Your Google Business Profile and website must match:

- same core services
- same service areas
- same contact details
- same business identity signals

Mismatch reduces trust and conversion quality.

## 3) Build local trust signals that buyers can verify

Minimum trust stack:

- real customer reviews
- review response cadence
- location-relevant proof or case snapshots
- clear phone, email, and service-area details

Trust signals improve both user confidence and local search performance.

## 4) Create page-level keyword clusters

Do not force all keywords into one page.

Create focused pages for your top service intents and naturally include:

- primary keyword in title and hero
- supporting terms in H2 sections
- internal links to relevant related guides

This improves topical relevance without keyword stuffing.

## 5) Track local SEO outcomes that matter

Monitor:

- calls from profile and website
- direction requests
- form enquiries
- booking starts and completions

If rankings improve but enquiries do not, conversion architecture needs work.

## Local SEO maintenance rhythm

Weekly:

- respond to new reviews
- update profile Q&A

Monthly:

- refresh profile photos
- audit key service pages
- review local search query patterns

Consistency usually beats random heavy optimization.

## Local citation and consistency checklist

Make sure your business details match across:

- website contact page
- Google Business Profile
- major directories and social profiles
- booking platforms and map listings

Use the exact same business name, phone, and address/service-area wording where possible. Inconsistent citations weaken local trust signals and can reduce ranking stability.

## On-page local SEO checklist for service pages

For each service page, verify:

- clear service and location intent in headline
- short local proof block
- visible contact action above the fold
- FAQ covering local buyer concerns

This improves both local ranking relevance and lead conversion quality.

## Final note

Local SEO is an operating process, not a one-time setup.

Get the basics right, maintain consistency, and your lead quality improves over time.
