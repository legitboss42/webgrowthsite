# Small Business Website Launch QA Checklist

A weak launch can damage trust in one day.

This website launch QA checklist keeps your go-live process practical, clear, and revenue-safe.

## Pre-launch checklist (must pass)

- Forms send to the right inbox.
- Auto-response or confirmation message works.
- CTA buttons point to live destinations.
- Phone and email links work on mobile.
- Core pages load acceptably on mobile data.

If one of these fails, delay launch and fix immediately.

## Technical checklist

- title and meta description exist for key pages
- canonical URLs are correct
- noindex is not accidentally applied to money pages
- redirects are set for old URLs
- sitemap and robots are accessible

These checks protect both SEO and crawl quality from day one.

## Trust and UX checklist

- hero message is specific and believable
- service promises are realistic
- pricing or scope expectations are clear
- contact path is visible without deep scroll
- legal pages are accessible

Trust breakdowns often come from missing basics, not complex UX issues.

## Tracking checklist

Before launch, verify:

- GA4 page views and conversion events
- pixel events for lead and checkout actions
- click-to-call and form completion tracking

After launch, verify again with real traffic.

## First 72-hour post-launch checklist

1. Monitor enquiry volume and form deliverability.
2. Review layout on real devices and browsers.
3. Check for 404s and unexpected redirects.
4. Confirm event tracking consistency.
5. Keep rollback notes if urgent fixes are required.

## Launch-day timeline template

- T-24h: final staging QA
- T-4h: backup and deployment prep
- T-0h: DNS/deploy switch
- T+1h: smoke test all core pages
- T+24h: analytics and conversion review

This creates accountability and reduces launch chaos.

## Launch communication checklist for client-facing teams

Before launch, align communication with everyone involved:

- who confirms final go-live approval
- who monitors inbox and lead flow on day one
- who handles urgent bug escalation
- what message is sent if minor issues appear

Many launch failures are communication failures, not technical failures. Define ownership early.

## SEO checks that should never be skipped at launch

Before go-live, verify:

- canonical URLs for core pages
- sitemap availability
- robots rules match your indexing strategy
- no accidental duplicate page variants

These checks protect your initial indexing quality and reduce avoidable ranking delays.

## Final note

Launch QA is not optional polish. It is revenue protection.

A clean launch compounds trust. A messy launch compounds firefighting.
