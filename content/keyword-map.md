## Editorial rules

- Article frontmatter `primaryKeyword` remains the source for the exact article target.
- Supporting articles link to the canonical commercial route rather than competing with it.
- Similar checklist pages must have distinct titles, openings, examples, and next actions.
- Claims remain evidence-safe even when a keyword suggests a result such as rankings, revenue, or conversions.
