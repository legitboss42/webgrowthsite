# Web Growth Claims Register

This register is the working source for claims review before public copy is published.

## Rules

- Verified metrics require a named project, measurement source, baseline, date range, and permission to publish.
- Qualitative outcomes describe what was designed, clarified, improved, or delivered without implying a measured business result.
- Illustrative examples must be labelled as examples and must not read like client evidence.
- Unsupported rankings, traffic, revenue, approval, conversion, speed, or appointment claims are removed or rewritten.

## Current high-risk items

| Surface | Risk | Required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `content/blog/stop-using-cheap-hosting.md` | Unsupported traffic, bounce, ranking, speed, vendor, and revenue claims | Use provider-neutral evaluation criteria and measured-baseline language. |
| Homepage proof sections | Unverified traffic, ranking, and revenue figures | Show verified project context or qualitative project notes only. |
| Service configurations | Outcome wording such as increased appointments or guaranteed conversion | Describe deliverables and intended decision support; do not promise results. |
| Portfolio and case studies | Qualitative notes presented too close to performance proof | Label scope, role, project type, and qualitative outcomes explicitly. |

## Approved wording pattern

Use: “This work is intended to support…” or “The delivered scope included…” when no measured outcome is available.

Avoid: “guarantees,” “will increase,” “3x faster,” “more revenue,” “higher rankings,” or specific performance figures without evidence.
