# Blog Improvement Report

## Scope

No new articles were created. The 36 existing Markdown articles were governed; 33 were approved and 3 were consolidated with redirects.

## Improvements

- Added required SEO title, primary keyword, search intent, and descriptive cover alt text.
- Enforced unique titles, SEO titles, and primary keywords across approved articles.
- Preserved visible FAQs, authors, reviewers, publication/review dates, takeaways, and related guides.
- Removed a sensational hosting headline and replaced it with a practical migration-focused title.
- Added missing contextual service links and corrected duplicate related links.
- Made the J Luxe announcement the visible series hub and restored the substantive series pages.

## Quality Gate

Each approved article must have a valid non-SVG cover, at least two FAQs, at least two approved related guides, three distinct contextual internal links, and a service link.

## Classification

- Google/AdSense policy: content must comply with publisher policies; Google does not prescribe a minimum article count or word count.
- SEO best practice: unique intent, descriptive metadata, internal links, and current review signals.
- Personal recommendation: do not add articles until production behavior and existing content performance are reviewed.
