# AdSense Rules

## AdSense Objective

The site should deserve approval because it is useful, trustworthy, easy to navigate, and content-first.

AdSense is a monetization layer, not the purpose of the website.

## Non-Negotiable Requirements

Web Growth must maintain substantial, visible pages for:

- About
- Contact
- Privacy Policy
- Terms
- Disclaimer
- Editorial Policy

These pages must not be thin or placeholder-like.

## Content Quality Requirements

All monetizable informational content must be:

- original
- helpful
- specific
- readable
- factually responsible
- clearly authored/reviewed where appropriate

Do not publish:

- scraped content
- spun content
- padded AI filler
- fake case studies
- invented statistics
- empty comparison pages
- doorway content

## Thin Content Warnings

Do not publish pages that exist only to:

- hold ads
- target a keyword with no substance
- capture traffic without helping the reader
- duplicate another page’s intent with slightly changed wording

Examples of high-risk thin content:

- weak glossary stubs
- underdeveloped tool pages
- empty category pages
- short “best X” posts with no firsthand value

## Layout Rules

The page must remain content-first.

Avoid:

- ads near misleading buttons
- ad-heavy above-the-fold layouts
- aggressive in-content interruptions
- commercial clutter on informational pages

## Ad Placement Rules

Ads may be considered later on:

- substantial Academy articles
- strong long-form informational guides
- high-traffic educational pages with clear reading flow

Ads should not be planned for:

- homepage hero
- contact page
- pricing page
- service pages
- portfolio pages
- thank-you pages
- internal tools
- form steps
- navigation areas

## No Deceptive Layouts

Never place ads so they can be confused with:

- CTAs
- navigation
- form buttons
- download buttons
- tool controls

## No Made-For-Ads Design

The redesign must not make the site feel like:

- an article farm
- an MFA content site
- a low-trust affiliate blog

The visual experience must still feel premium and useful even with no ads visible.

## Academy Monetization Rules

Academy content should follow this ladder:

1. satisfy search intent
2. build trust
3. create internal paths
4. optionally monetize

Monetization order:

- service CTA
- lead magnet / digital product
- relevant affiliate mention if justified
- AdSense as secondary

## Trust Signals That Help

- named founder and editorial presence
- reviewed/update dates
- visible contact info
- substantial legal pages
- clear page purpose
- credible case studies and examples

## Repo-Specific Guidance

- Keep `ads.txt` present at the root.
- Global AdSense script is already in layout; do not treat that as proof that the site is ready everywhere.
- Internal/noindex utility routes must never become monetization targets.
- Campaign pages like `/launch/`, `/website-build/`, and `/hosting-offer/` should stay focused on conversion, not display ads.

## Approval Readiness Standard

Before an AdSense application, verify:

- no visible low-value pages in core navigation
- no thin Academy articles
- About/Contact/Privacy/Terms/Disclaimer/Editorial pages are all complete
- site structure is coherent
- mobile UX is strong
- no deceptive ad placements are planned
