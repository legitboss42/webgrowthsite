Small Business Website Launch QA Checklist

1. Verify form submissions reach inbox.
2. Verify confirmation and autoresponder behavior.
3. Test all CTA buttons and contact links.
4. Validate mobile layout on key pages.
5. Validate title/meta/canonical on launch pages.
6. Confirm no accidental noindex on target pages.
7. Validate GA4 and pixel lead events.
8. Validate redirects for changed URLs.
9. Confirm legal and trust pages are accessible.
10. Monitor first 72 hours for regressions.
